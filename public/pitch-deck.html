01 • THE PROBLEM LANDSCAPE • 課題の背景

Why Amateur & Citizen Astronomy Tools Fail Today

Stargazers, astrophotographers, and students face four critical barriers between capturing the night sky and extracting trustworthy scientific insight.

1. AI Hallucination in Astrophotography

Consumer AI "night mode" enhancers invent fake stars and synthetic nebulae, destroying photometric integrity and making images scientifically unusable.

Need: Non-destructive restoration + original vs. restored source cross-matching
2. Opaque "Black-Box" Weather Scores

Existing stargazing apps show arbitrary visibility percentages without disclosing mathematical weights, sensor limitations, or forecast vs. live telemetry distinctions.

Need: Transparent heuristic formula + dynamic weight rebalancing
3. Fragmented Ephemeris & Multi-Spectral Data

Checking lunar phases (USNO), eclipse totality (IMCCE), rocket launches, and multi-wavelength sky surveys (NASA SkyView) requires switching across 6+ legacy portals.

Need: Unified sanctuary console with local timezone normalization
4. Unverified Community Sky Catalogs

Astronomy leaderboards are flooded with AI-generated art or downloaded screenshots with stripped metadata rather than genuine optical camera captures.

Need: Hardware EXIF + GPS + Night-Window verification backed by Supabase

03 • CHAPTER III: SPIRIT LENS • 鏡花水月 • 天体候補検出

Real-ESRGAN Restoration & Photutils Source Detection

Separates display transformations (`linear`, `percentile`, `log`, `asinh`) from raw floating-point photometric measurements (`ADU`) and cross-matches original vs. AI-restored frames.

5-Stage Scientific Photometry Pipeline
  • 1. Validation & Decoding: Supports JPEG, PNG, WebP, TIFF & astronomical FITS HDUs (astropy.io.fits), masking alpha transparency.
  • 2. 2D Spatially Varying Background: Background2D + MedianBackground with SigmaClip(sigma=3.0) on adaptive meshes to remove sky gradients & vignetting.
  • 3. Point-Source (Star) Detection: DAOStarFinder + circular aperture photometry (CircularAperture) for unresolved stellar peaks.
  • 4. Extended-Source (Galaxy) Segmentation: 2D Gaussian PSF convolution + detect_sources + deblend_sources + SourceCatalog moment analysis.
  • 5. Original vs. Restored Cross-Match: Flags restored_only sources to prevent AI hallucinations from passing as real stars.
Per-Candidate Measured Properties
ASTROMETRY & FLUX
Centroid (x, y), BBox, Peak ADU, Segment & Aperture Flux, SNR
MORPHOLOGY
FWHM (px), Area (px²), Semi-Major/Minor (a, b), Ellipticity (1−b/a)
Classifications: Star-Like Point Candidate | Extended-Source Candidate | Possible Galaxy | Uncertain / Artifact

Includes interactive HTML5 Canvas zoom/pan overlays, 160×160 candidate cutout inspector, and 1-click CSV / JSON catalog export.

04 • CHAPTERS I, II & IV: REAL ASTRONOMICAL TELEMETRY • 望楼と夜の誉れ

Authoritative Public APIs & Transparent Scoring

Zero hardcoded dates or fabricated weather—every metric is dynamically fetched, cached with TTL resilience, and converted from UTC to the observer's local timezone.

US Naval Observatory
USNO v4.0

Fetches official primary lunar phase milestones (`New Moon`, `First Quarter`, `Full Moon`, `Last Quarter`) and computes instantaneous illumination % and lunar age in days.

aa.usno.navy.mil/api/moon/phases/year
IMCCE OPALE Ephemeris
INPOP19A

Retrieves calculated lunar eclipses (`Total`, `Partial`, `Penumbral`), umbral magnitude, P1/P2 contact timestamps, and zenith coordinates at greatest eclipse.

opale.imcce.fr/api/v1/phenomena/eclipses
Open-Meteo NWP Model
Hourly NWP

Queries dynamic coordinate cloud cover (%), 2m relative humidity (%), and optical visibility (m). Explicitly labeled as NWP forecast rather than live telescope feed.

api.open-meteo.com/v1/forecast
Transparent Night Honor Score Mathematical Model
Night Honor Score = (0.53 × ClearSky) + (0.29 × VisibilityScore) + (0.18 × HumidityScore)

NASA SkyView Virtual Observatory & Transient Lab

Accessible from any chapter via floating right-edge drawers, bringing professional multi-spectral archival astronomy and relativistic Doppler spectroscopy into the browser.
